# Code of Conduct

This project follows the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1, summarized here in
plain English. The full text is available at
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/

## Our pledge

Everyone who takes part in this project, in any role, promises to make
participation a harassment-free experience for all people, regardless of age,
body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that help this community: being kind and respectful of
different viewpoints, giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully,
taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them, and focusing on
what is best for the project and the people it serves.

Examples of behavior that do not help: sexualized language or imagery, trolling
and insulting comments, personal or political attacks, public or private
harassment, publishing private information without permission, and other
conduct that a reasonable person would consider unprofessional.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject any contribution or behavior
that violates this pledge, and will explain their decision when it is
appropriate to do so. If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior,
report it using the contact information in SECURITY.md.

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
brought to the project maintainers, who are committed to a fair and honest
enforcement process.

This code of conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1,
available at https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
